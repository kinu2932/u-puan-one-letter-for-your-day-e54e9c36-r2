## The entry

The program (`main.py`) writes exactly one character — the letter **D** — to `/output/entry.txt`. D stands for **Doomscrolled**: the day spent lost in an endless scroll, phone in hand, thumb moving long after midnight.

## Why D

Winner-profile-first, from the live field (3,179 submissions, 24 Nov 2026):

- Census: Z 525, D 232, K 203 — the tired family dominates, and D is its sharpest word.
- The published judge brief weighs vivid/specific first ("a sharp picture of the day rather than a vague mood"), then funny/surprising, then true. "Doomscrolled" paints a scene; "Tired" / "Exhausted" are vague moods the brief ranks below a specific picture.
- D beats Z head-to-head on vividness, humour and truth, with far fewer copies sharing its rate (232 vs 525).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes one byte, `D`, no newline, to `/output/entry.txt`.
